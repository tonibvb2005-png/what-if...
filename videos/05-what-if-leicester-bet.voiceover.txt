VOICEOVER – "What if you bet £1,000 on Leicester?"
One audio file per line -> audio/what-if-leicester/01.mp3 … 13.mp3
Voice: energetic storyteller, a bit cheeky. Build tension from line 7 to 10, explode at 11.
Tip: a tiny pause (0.2 s) at the start and end of each file is fine – the video adapts to the length.

01  What if you bet one thousand pounds on Leicester City to win the Premier League?
02  August 2015. He walks into the bookies and puts one thousand pounds on Leicester. The odds? Five thousand to one.
03  His mates can't stop laughing. Leicester almost got relegated last season!
04  But he just smiles. He knows something they don't.
05  The new manager? Claudio Ranieri. Even Gary Lineker tweets: "Claudio Ranieri? Really?"
06  Then Jamie Vardy starts scoring. And scoring. Eleven games in a row. A new Premier League record.
07  Christmas 2015. Leicester are top of the league. His mates stop laughing.
08  February. Leicester smash Manchester City three-one. Away from home.
09  Then his phone rings. The bookies want to buy back his bet. His mates beg him to take the money. He says no.
10  May 2016. If Tottenham don't win at Chelsea, Leicester are champions... Chelsea two. Tottenham two.
11  Leicester City are champions of England!
12  So how much does he win? One thousand pounds at five thousand to one... Five. Million. Pounds.
13  What would you do with five million? Follow for more What Ifs.
