# Video #007 „What if a king paid you one gold coin for every dead rat?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie bisher), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 45–50 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- **Stimmung:** Märchen-Erzähler, trocken und augenzwinkernd. Klingt wie eine Gute-Nacht-Geschichte, in der alles schiefgeht.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[curious, storyteller] What if a king paid you one gold coin for every dead rat?

[matter-of-fact] The kingdom of Mudbury has a problem. [beat] Ten thousand rats.

[proud, royal] So the king makes a deal. [announcing] Bring me a rat tail… get one gold coin.

[excited, fast] Everyone grabs a broom. Rats everywhere. Coins everywhere.

[suspicious, slower] But one rat catcher does something strange. [short pause] He builds… a rat farm.

[deadpan] He feeds them cheese. [beat] Lots of cheese.

[amused] The neighbors laugh. [mocking, laughing] "You're supposed to kill them!"

[serious] The royal treasurer is furious. [shouting, panicked] "The gold is gone! And there are more rats than ever!"

[flat] So the king cancels the deal.

[deadpan] The rat catcher shrugs… [sly] and opens the cages.

[dramatic] Now Mudbury has thirty thousand rats.

[serious, slower] This really happened. Hanoi, nineteen oh two. People bred rats just for the reward.

[softly, smiling] Today it's called the cobra effect. Pay for a problem… and you get more of it.
```
