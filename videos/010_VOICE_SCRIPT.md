# Video #010 „What if someone tried to sell ice… in the tropics?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie bisher), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 45–50 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- **Stimmung:** Wie #008/#009, also lebendig und emotional. Bei „Drip. Drip. Drip.“ langsam und dramatisch, bei der Insel-Party begeistert.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[curious, playful] What if someone tried to sell ice… [amused] in the tropics?

[matter-of-fact] Boston, eighteen oh six. Frederic Tudor cuts giant blocks from a frozen pond.

[proud] His plan? Ship them to the Caribbean. [dramatic] Where it's boiling hot.

[amused] Boston laughs. [mocking] The newspaper calls it "a slippery speculation."

[calm] The ship sails for weeks. [worried] And the ice melts. [slow, dramatic] Drip. Drip. Drip.

[confused] On the island, nobody knows what to do with ice.

[serious] His investors are furious. [shouting, angry] "You sold us a puddle!"

[deadpan] Tudor just shrugs… [sly] and packs the ice in sawdust.

[excited, building] Now it survives. Cold drinks. Ice cream. [cheering] The island goes crazy.

[impressed] He ships ice all the way to India. [proud] They call him the Ice King.

[serious, slower] Without him? [ominous] Warm drinks. For decades.

[softly, smiling] So next time ice clinks in your glass… thank the man everyone laughed at.
```
