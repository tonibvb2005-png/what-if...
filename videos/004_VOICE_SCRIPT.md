# Video #004 „What if one man had said yes?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie #001/#003), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 44–50 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- **Stimmung:** klaustrophobisch und gedrückt, wie unter Wasser. Bei den Explosionen schneller, beim Auftauchen fast ehrfürchtig.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[low, tense, documentary narrator] What if one man had said yes… deep under the ocean?

[matter-of-fact] Caribbean, nineteen sixty-two. A Soviet submarine hides near Cuba.

[heavy, tired] The air is hot. Over forty-five degrees. The batteries are dying.

[sudden, urgent] Then: explosions. American ships drop depth charges right above them.

[tense, quieter] No contact with Moscow. [ominous] Maybe the war has already started.

[serious] The captain is furious. [shouting, angry] "Load the nuclear torpedo!"

[slow, deliberate] But launching needs three officers to agree. [beat] Two say yes.

[very calm] One says no.

[calm, firm] He keeps calm and argues: [quietly] "Those are signals. We surface."

[awed, slow] The submarine rises… right into the middle of the American fleet. [beat] Nobody fires.

[serious, slower] If he had said yes? [ominous] A nuclear blast at sea could have started World War Three.

[warm, reveal] His name was Vasili Arkhipov.

[softly, with a smile] Most people have never heard of him… until now.
```
