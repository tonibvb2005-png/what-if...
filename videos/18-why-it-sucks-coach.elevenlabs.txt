[deadpan] Why it sucks being the coach.

It starts when nobody else volunteers. [pause] Now you own twenty bibs, thirty cones... and a group chat with forty parents.

Saturday, seven a.m. You're the first one there. You put up the nets. [pause] In the rain.

Match day. Fifteen players. Eleven positions. [pause] Fifteen parents who think their kid should start.

You lose three nil? [pause] "Bad tactics." [pause] You win three nil? [pause] "Great players."

Second half. A dad on the sideline shouts different instructions. [pause] Louder than you.

Half-time. You prepared the perfect speech. [pause] First question: "Can we have orange slices?"

End of the season. They win the league! [pause] Everyone gets in the team photo. [pause] You're the one holding the camera.

That's the thing about being the coach. No money. No thanks.

[softly] And next Saturday... you're there at seven again.

[warm] Tag your coach. Tell them thank you.
