[deadpan] Why it sucks being a striker.

It starts when you're six. You score ten goals a game. [pause] Everyone loves you.

Age nine. The other team finds out. [pause] Now three defenders follow you everywhere.

Age eleven. You score twice. You miss one easy chance. [pause] In the car, your dad only talks about the miss.

Age thirteen. Eighty minutes. Nobody passes to you. Not once. [pause] Coach: "Why aren't you involved?"

Age fifteen. Last minute. Penalty. Everyone is watching. [pause] You hit the bar.

Age sixteen. Hat-trick! [pause] Next week, you don't score. [pause] "Flop." "Bench him." "Sell him."

Age seventeen. You finally score the winner. You run to the fans... [pause] Offside.

That's the thing about being a striker. Everyone else is judged on ninety minutes.

[softly] You're judged on one second.

[warm] Tag your striker. Tell them: the next one goes in.
