[deadpan] Why it sucks being the captain.

It starts when you're eight. The coach gives you the armband. [pause] You think it's an honour. [pause] It means you do the coin toss... and lose it.

Age eleven. You're losing five nil. The coach says: "Captain, say something." [pause] Everyone stares at you.

Age thirteen. Your teammate screams at the referee. [pause] The referee: "Captain! Come here." [pause] You get the yellow card.

Age fifteen. Final whistle. Everyone goes home. [pause] You collect the bibs. The cones. The balls. Alone.

Age sixteen. Penalty shootout. Nobody wants the fifth one. [pause] Everyone looks at the armband.

Age seventeen. You win the cup! [pause] You lift the trophy for one second... [pause] then the striker takes it for the photo.

That's the thing about being the captain. When you win, it's the team.

[softly] When you lose... it's you.

[warm] Tag your captain. Tell them: we've got you.
