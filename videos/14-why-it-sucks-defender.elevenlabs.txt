[deadpan] Why it sucks being a defender.

It starts when you're six. You're the tallest kid on the team. [pause] So the coach says: "Just stand at the back."

Age nine. Corner kick. You finally get to go up. [pause] The ball never comes. [pause] You jog all the way back.

Age twelve. Perfect sliding tackle. Clean ball. [pause] Nobody claps.

Age fourteen. The striker falls over your shadow. Penalty. [pause] Yellow card... for you.

Age sixteen. Ninety minutes. Not one shot on target. Clean sheet. [pause] Man of the match? [pause] The goalkeeper.

Age seventeen. The new winger is faster than a scooter. You blink... [pause] and the ball goes through your legs.

One nutmeg. [pause] Ten million views.

That's the thing about being a defender. When you do your job... nothing happens.
