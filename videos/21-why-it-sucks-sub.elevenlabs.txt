[deadpan] Why it sucks being the sub.

It starts when you're seven. Everyone gets a shirt. [pause] Yours says "fourteen".

Age nine. You warm up for the whole first half. [pause] Second half... you warm up again.

Age eleven. Your parents drive you two hours to the away game. [pause] You play four minutes. [pause] Including stoppage time.

Age thirteen. Freezing cold. You're on the bench with a blanket. [pause] The starters are sweating.

Age fifteen. The coach finally looks at you. You jump up. [pause] He says: "Pass me the water bottle."

Age sixteen. Eighty-ninth minute. You come on! [pause] First touch... [pause] final whistle.

Age seventeen. You come off the bench and score the winner! [pause] Next week? [pause] Back on the bench. "We don't change a winning team."

That's the thing about being the sub. You're always ready.

[softly] Nobody's ready for you.
