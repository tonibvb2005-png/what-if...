## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[curious, documentary narrator] What if doctors had never started washing their hands?

[matter-of-fact] Vienna, eighteen forty-seven. One of the biggest hospitals in Europe.

[concerned] In one ward, mothers keep getting sick. [serious] Far more than next door.

[thoughtful] A young doctor notices why. [lower, slightly ominous] The doctors come straight from the autopsy room… to the mothers. [pause] Without washing.

[determined] So he makes everyone scrub their hands in chlorine water.

[amused] The other doctors laugh. [mocking, posh] "A gentleman's hands are always clean!"

[serious] His boss is furious. [shouting, angry] "Stop this NONSENSE!"

[deadpan] He shrugs. [short pause] And keeps scrubbing.

[building up] Within months, deaths in his ward drop from one in ten… [impressed] to around one in a hundred.

[serious, slower] Without him? [ominous] Germs would keep jumping from hand to hand for decades longer.

[warm, reveal] His name was Ignaz Semmelweis.

[softly, smiling] Today, he's called the saviour of mothers.
```
