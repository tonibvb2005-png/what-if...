Age ten. You run twelve kilometres. The striker runs two. [pause] He still gets the first drink at half-time.

Age twelve. You win the ball. You look up. Nobody is moving. [pause] So you pass it sideways. [pause] Coach: "Be more creative!"

Age fourteen. Every game, you get kicked. Again. And again. [pause] The referee: "Play on."

Age sixteen. Thirty yards out. You shoot... [pause] top corner! [pause] Now every time you touch the ball, the whole team screams: "PASS IT!"

Age seventeen. Fifteen assists this season. [pause] The highlight video? [pause] Fifteen goals... by the striker.

That's the thing about being a midfielder. Everyone wants the ball.
