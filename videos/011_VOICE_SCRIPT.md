# Video #011 „What if a man cut his own elevator rope… on purpose?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie bisher), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 45–52 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- **Stimmung:** Wie #008–#010, also lebendig und emotional. Beim Hochfahren steigernd und spannend, bei „Snap!“ ein kurzer Schock, danach die Erleichterung.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[curious, playful] What if a man cut his own elevator rope… [amused] on purpose?

[matter-of-fact] New York, eighteen fifty-four. Nobody trusts elevators. [ominous] If the rope snaps… you fall.

[intrigued] So a mechanic named Elisha Otis builds a strange platform. [mysterious] With iron teeth on the sides.

[building tension] At the World's Fair, he climbs on. [slow] Up. [higher] Up. [tense] Higher.

[amused] The crowd laughs. [mocking, laughing] "He's going to die!"

[serious] A fair official is furious. [shouting, angry] "Get down from there!"

[deadpan] Otis just shrugs… [calm, cool] and tells his helper: "Cut the rope."

[shocked, sharp] Snap! [gasping] The platform drops… [beat] [relieved] and stops. Right in mid-air.

[calm, proud, gentleman] "All safe, gentlemen. All safe."

[excited, building] Suddenly, buildings can go higher. Ten floors. Fifty. [triumphant] A hundred.

[serious, slower] Without his brake? [ominous] No skyscrapers. Cities stay flat.

[softly, smiling] So next time you step into an elevator… thank the man who cut his own rope.
```
