# ElevenLabs script: "What if you bet £1,000 on Leicester?"

**Model:** Eleven v3, because it understands the audio tags in [square brackets].
With an older model (Multilingual v2, Turbo, Flash), delete the [tags], otherwise they are read aloud.
**Voice:** a male British storyteller, warm and a bit cheeky (for example a "narrator" or "storyteller" voice from the Voice Library).
**Settings (guide values):** Stability about 40 %, Similarity about 75 %, Style about 30 %, Speed 1.0–1.05.
**Export:** one file per line, named `01.mp3` … `13.mp3`, then send them to me.

---

### 01 – Hook (curious, draws you in)
Direction: start straight away with no breath first. Stress "one thousand" and leave a short pause before "Stay".
```
[curious] What if you bet one thousand pounds on Leicester City... to win the Premier League? [pause] Stay until the end.
```

### 02 – At the bookies (relaxed storytelling, then a punchline)
Direction: calm at first. A short dramatic pause before "Five thousand to one", which then lands slowly and clearly.
```
August twenty fifteen. He walks into the bookies and puts one thousand pounds on Leicester. [pause] The odds? [dramatic] Five thousand... to one.
```

### 03 – The friends laugh (amused, a little mocking)
Direction: the narrator laughs along. "Almost got relegated" is said with a disbelieving grin.
```
[laughs] His mates can't stop laughing. [amused] Leicester almost got relegated last season!
```

### 04 – He stays confident (quieter, conspiratorial)
Direction: noticeably quieter and slower, as if telling a secret, with a smile in the voice.
```
[softly] But he just smiles. [pause] He knows something they don't.
```

### 05 – Ranieri (dry, ironic)
Direction: read "Claudio Ranieri? Really?" as a sceptical quote, stretching "Really" a little.
```
The new manager? Claudio Ranieri. [sarcastic] Even Gary Linneker tweets: "Claudio Ranieri? ...Really?"
```

### 06 – Vardy (getting faster, rising excitement)
Direction: "And scoring" is a notch more excited than "starts scoring". "Eleven games in a row" is clearly stressed and almost proud.
```
Then Jamie Vardy starts scoring. [excited] And scoring. [pause] Eleven games in a row! A new Premier League record.
```

### 07 – Christmas (festive, then a dry punchline)
Direction: the first sentences sound warm and happy. "His mates stop laughing" is short, dry and slightly malicious.
```
Christmas, twenty fifteen. Leicester are top of the league. [pause] [deadpan] His mates stop laughing.
```

### 08 – Man City (powerful, energetic)
Direction: "smash" with punch. "Away from home" is added on as an exclamation.
```
February. [excited] Leicester smash Manchester City, three one. [pause] Away from home!
```

### 09 – Cash-out (rising tension, then calm)
Direction: urgent and a little faster up to "take the money". Then a pause, and "He says no." is very calm, short and confident.
```
[tense] Then his phone rings. The bookies want to buy back his bet. [urgent] His mates beg him to take the money. [pause] [calm] He says no.
```

### 10 – Chelsea vs Tottenham (very tense, almost whispered)
Direction: quieter and slower, building suspense. A long pause before the result. "Chelsea two... Tottenham two." is spoken slowly and with emphasis.
```
[whispers] May, twenty sixteen. If Tottenham don't win at Chelsea... Leicester are champions. [long pause] Chelsea... two. [pause] Tottenham... two.
```

### 11 – Champions (explosion, shouting with joy)
Direction: full volume, euphoric, like a football commentator.
```
[shouting] [excited] LEICESTER CITY ARE CHAMPIONS OF ENGLAND!
```

### 12 – The payout (suspense, then the big reveal)
Direction: calm and teasing at the start. A long pause before "Five. Million. Pounds.", with each word stressed on its own.
```
[curious] So... how much does he win? One thousand pounds, at five thousand to one... [long pause] [excited] Five. Million. Pounds.
```

### 13 – Call to action (friendly, smiling)
Direction: relaxed, inviting, with a smile in the voice.
```
[playful] What would you do with five million? [pause] Follow for more What Ifs.
```

---

**Tips**
- If a line sounds wrong, generate it 2–3 times and pick the best take. Eleven v3 varies quite a lot.
- If the [pause] tags don't work in your model: three dots `...` also create a short pause, and a full stop plus a new sentence creates a longer one.
- "Lineker" is already written as `Linneker` so the voice pronounces it correctly.
