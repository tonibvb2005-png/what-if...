## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[dramatic, documentary narrator] What if one man hadn't nailed copper to the bottom of his ship?

[matter-of-fact] Seventeen seventy-nine. A British shipyard.

[dry, amused] The shipwright covers the whole hull in shiny copper plates. Thousands of them.

[amused] The sailors laugh. [mocking] "Is she going to a royal ball?"

[serious] The captain is furious. [shouting, angry] "That copper costs a fortune!"

[deadpan] He shrugs. [short pause] And keeps hammering.

[mysterious] Months later, in the warm Caribbean, the other ships get… guests.

[creepy, slightly faster] Barnacles. Seaweed. And shipworms that eat the wood from the inside.

[slow, heavy] The ships get slower. [beat] And slower.

[sudden, urgent] Then: an enemy fleet. Every ship runs. [tense] But only one is fast enough.

[satisfied] Nothing sticks to copper. His hull is still smooth.

[serious, slower] Without the copper? [ominous] A worm-eaten hull can sink a warship.

[warm, smiling] That's why we still call something reliable… copper-bottomed.
```
