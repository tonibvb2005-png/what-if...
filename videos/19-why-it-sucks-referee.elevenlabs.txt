[deadpan] Why it sucks being the referee.

Your very first game. Under nines. [pause] A grandma calls you blind.

You give a free kick. Half the crowd hates you. [pause] You don't give it... the other half hates you.

Ninety minutes. You run ten kilometres. [pause] Nobody even passes to you.

You make a hundred decisions. Ninety-nine are right. [pause] Everyone talks about the other one.

Last minute. Penalty! [pause] Suddenly, eleven players are screaming in your face.

No VAR. No replay. Just you... [pause] and two hundred phones filming.

After the game, you walk to your car. Alone. [pause] Someone shouts: "Get home safe, ref!" [pause] You're not sure if it's nice.

That's the thing about being the referee. Without you... there is no game.

[softly] And everyone forgets that.
