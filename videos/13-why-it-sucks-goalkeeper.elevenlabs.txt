[deadpan] Why it sucks being a goalkeeper.

It starts when you're six. Everyone wants to score. You're the slowest kid on the team. [pause] So the coach puts you in goal.

Age eight. Forty minutes. Nothing happens. You're freezing. Nobody passes to you.

After the game, your dad asks: "Did you save anything?" [pause] You touched the ball twice.

Age eleven. You make ten saves. You still lose one nil. [pause] And everyone looks at you.

Age thirteen. A shot from five yards. You save it... [pause] with your face. Everyone laughs.

Age fifteen. Training ends. The others play five-a-side. You? Thirty crosses. In the rain. Alone.

Age seventeen. Ninetieth minute. One nil up. Easy catch... [pause] and it slips through your gloves.

[softly] Suddenly, everybody knows your name.

That's the thing about being a goalkeeper. A striker misses ten chances, scores one... and he's a hero.

You make ten saves, drop one... and you're the villain.

[warm] Tag your goalkeeper. Tell them thank you.
