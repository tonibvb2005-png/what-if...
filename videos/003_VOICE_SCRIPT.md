# Video #003 „What if one man had followed the rules?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie #001), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 44–50 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- Dieses Video lebt von **Spannung**: am Anfang leise und dicht, beim Alarm schneller, beim Warten fast flüsternd.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[low, tense, documentary narrator] What if one man had followed the rules… the night the world almost ended?

[matter-of-fact, quiet] Near Moscow, nineteen eighty-three. A secret bunker.

[sudden, urgent] Suddenly, the screen flashes red. Launch detected. [faster] An American missile. [beat] Then two. [beat] Then five.

[serious, cold] The rules say: report it. And Moscow strikes back.

[tense] Everyone stares at the duty officer. [shouting, panicked] "Sir! We have to report it!"

[calm, slower] He doesn't move. [thoughtful] Why would America attack with only five missiles?

[very calm] He picks up the phone… [pause] and says: [firm] false alarm.

[whispering, tense] Then they wait. Twenty-three minutes. [relieved] Nothing hits.

[matter-of-fact] The satellite had mistaken sunlight on clouds for rocket engines.

[serious, slower] If he had followed the rules? [ominous] It could have started a nuclear war.

[warm, reveal] His name was Stanislav Petrov.

[dry, amused] He saved the world… and got in trouble for his paperwork.
```
