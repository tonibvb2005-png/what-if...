# Video #012 „What if firefighters had to take the stairs?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie bisher), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 48–55 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- **Stimmung:** Wie #008–#011, also lebendig und emotional. Beim Feueralarm hektisch, bei „Whoosh!“ und „Slide. Slide. Slide.“ rasant und rhythmisch.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[curious, playful] What if firefighters had to take the stairs?

[matter-of-fact] Chicago, eighteen seventy-eight. [urgent] The fire bell rings. [fast, chaotic] Firefighters sprint down the stairs… [amused] and trip over each other.

[intrigued] Upstairs, a firefighter named George Reid grabs a wooden pole… used to lift hay. [excited] And slides down.

[fast, thrilled] Whoosh! He beats everyone to the horses.

[thoughtful] His captain, David Kenyon, gets an idea. [sly] He cuts a hole in the floor… and puts in a pole.

[amused] The other stations laugh. [mocking] They call it "Kenyon's Folly."

[serious] The fire chief is furious. [shouting, angry] "You cut a hole in my firehouse?!"

[deadpan] Kenyon just shrugs… [cool] and rings the bell.

[fast, rhythmic] Slide. Slide. Slide. [proud] His team is out the door first. Every single time.

[uplifting] Soon, fire stations everywhere copy him. [warm, slower] Seconds saved… lives saved.

[serious, slower] Without the pole? [ominous] Firefighters stuck on the stairs. While the fire grows.

[softly, smiling] So next time you see a fire pole… remember the hole everybody laughed at.
```
