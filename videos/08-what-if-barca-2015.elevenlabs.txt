[curious] What if you gave up on Barça... in January twenty fifteen?

January fourth. Barcelona lose one nil at Real Sociedad. Messi and Neymar start on the bench.

[tense] The next day, Messi misses training. The papers say he wants out. And the sporting director gets sacked.

[deadpan] His mate has seen enough. He sells his Barça shirt. [pause] For ten euros.

[softly] But our guy keeps his. Because Barça have a secret weapon. [pause] Actually... three of them.

[building] Messi. Swarez. Neymar. [pause] The striker who got banned for biting a player at the World Cup... is now part of the scariest front three on the planet.

[excited] May twenty fifteen. Bayern Munich. Messi goes past Bwahteng, who falls to the ground... and chips it over Noyer.

[excited] They win the league. [pause] Then, in the cup final, Messi dribbles past half of Athletic Bilbao... and scores.

[tense] June sixth. Berlin. The Champions League final, against Juventus.

[building] Rakitich scores. Morata equalises. [pause] Then Swarez. [pause] And Neymar, in the very last minute.

[shouting] BARCELONA WIN THE TREBLE! The first club ever to do it twice!

[amazed] Messi, Swarez and Neymar scored one hundred and twenty-two goals that season.

[playful] And his mate? [pause] He tried to buy his shirt back... for one hundred euros. [pause] Follow for more What Ifs.
