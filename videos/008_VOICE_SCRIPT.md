# Video #008 „What if your job was shoving strangers into a train?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie bisher), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 42–48 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- **Stimmung:** Energiegeladener und emotionaler als sonst. Bei „Push!“ rhythmisch wie ein Trainer, beim Quetschen fast lachend, bei „Without the pushers?“ kurz ernst.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[curious, playful] What if your job was shoving strangers into a train?

[matter-of-fact] Tokyo, nineteen fifty-five. [building] Millions of people. Every single morning.

[stressed, fast] The train is full. The doors won't close. [exasperated] Nobody gets to work.

[sly] So the station hires students… [proud] in white gloves.

[energetic, like a coach] Their job? Push. [louder] Push harder. [shouting, laughing] Squeeze!

[amused, almost laughing] Faces get squished against the glass.

[serious] A businessman is furious. [shouting, angry] "Don't touch my suit!"

[deadpan] The pusher just bows… [cheeky] and pushes again.

[satisfied, rhythmic] The doors close. The train leaves. [proud] Exactly on time.

[serious, slower] Without the pushers? [ominous] Trains stuck. The whole city late for work.

[warm, informative] They're called oshiya. And some stations in Tokyo still use them today.

[softly, smiling] So next time your train is packed… be glad nobody's pushing you.
```
