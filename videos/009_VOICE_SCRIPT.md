# Video #009 „What if your alarm clock was a woman with a pea shooter?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie bisher), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 45–50 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- **Stimmung:** Wie bei #008, also lebendig und emotional. Am Anfang leise wie im Morgengrauen, bei „Puff. Ping!“ verspielt-rhythmisch, beim wütenden Mann laut.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[curious, playful] What if your alarm clock was a woman with a pea shooter?

[matter-of-fact] London. Alarm clocks cost a fortune. [serious] And if you're late for the factory… [harsh] you're fired.

[warm] So people pay Mary Smith sixpence a week… [amused] to wake them up.

[quiet, early morning] Before dawn, she walks the street with a long tube.

[playful, rhythmic] Puff. [bright] Ping! [playful] Puff. [bright] Ping! [amused] Dried peas against the windows.

[excited] Inside, workers jump out of bed.

[amused] The neighbors laugh. [mocking, laughing] "She's shooting peas at houses!"

[serious] One man is furious. [shouting, angry] "You woke the wrong house!"

[deadpan] Mary just shrugs… [sly] and reloads.

[uplifting, building] Every window lights up. The whole street walks to work. [proud] Right on time.

[serious, slower] Without her? [ominous] Thousands asleep. Machines silent. No pay.

[warm, informative] They were called knocker-uppers. Cheap alarm clocks finally put them out of work.

[softly, smiling] So next time you hit snooze… be glad it's not a pea to the window.
```
