## 3. Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[calm, documentary narrator] What if the Romans had built their roads… flat?

[matter-of-fact] Rome. Three hundred twelve BC. Soldiers are building a new road.

[dry] But the engineer wants it curved. [slightly amused] Like a turtle's back. With a ditch on each side.

[amused] The soldiers laugh. [mocking, laughing] "A road with a hump? The carts will roll off!"

[serious] The centurion is furious. [shouting, angry] "Roads are flat! Make it FLAT!"

[deadpan] He shrugs. [short pause] And keeps building. Layer after layer.

[ominous, lower voice] Then the winter storms come.

[dramatic] The old dirt roads turn into rivers of mud. [grim] Carts sink.

[calm, satisfied] But on his road, the rain just runs off… into the ditches.

[proud] The legion marches straight through.

[serious, slower] Without the curve? [ominous] Water soaks in, and the road falls apart in a few winters.

[warm, reveal] That road was the Via Appia.

[softly, smiling] And you can still walk on it today.
```
