What if the Romans had built their roads flat?
Rome. Three hundred twelve BC. Soldiers are building a new road.
But the engineer wants it curved. Like a turtle's back. With a ditch on each side.
The soldiers laugh. "A road with a hump? The carts will roll off!"
The centurion is furious. "Roads are flat! Make it flat!"
He shrugs. And keeps building. Layer after layer.
Then the winter storms come.
The old dirt roads turn into rivers of mud. Carts sink.
But on his road, the rain just runs off into the ditches.
The legion marches straight through.
Without the curve? Water soaks in, and the road falls apart in a few winters.
That road was the Via Appia.
And you can still walk on it today.
