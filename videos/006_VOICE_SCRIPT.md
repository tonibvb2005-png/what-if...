# Video #006 „What if one tailor hadn't hammered horse rivets into a pair of pants?“: Sprecher-Skript für ElevenLabs

## Stimme & Einstellungen
- **Stimme:** Brian (wie bisher), gleiche Einstellungen · **Modell:** Eleven v3 · **Stability:** Natural
- **Ziel-Länge:** 45–52 Sekunden · **Export:** eine MP3/WAV-Datei
- **Stimmung:** Western-Erzähler, trocken und augenzwinkernd. Beim Pferde-Test **spannend**, mit Pausen wie vor einem Duell.
- Tags in `[…]` werden nicht gesprochen. **Text nicht umformulieren**, sonst passen die Untertitel nicht mehr.

## Skript zum Reinkopieren (Eleven v3)

```
[curious, documentary narrator] What if one tailor hadn't hammered horse rivets into a pair of pants?

[matter-of-fact] Nevada, eighteen seventy-one. Gold miners keep ripping their pockets.

[dry, rhythmic] Heavy tools. Heavy gold. [sharp] Rip. [amused] Everything falls out.

[dry] So the tailor grabs the copper rivets he uses for horse blankets… and hammers them into the pockets.

[amused] The cowboys laugh. [mocking, laughing] "Metal in your pants? Are you a horse?"

[serious] The town tailor is furious. [shouting, angry] "That's not how you make pants!"

[deadpan] He shrugs. [short pause] And keeps hammering.

[tense, slow] Then: the test. [beat] Two horses. [beat] One pair of pants. [tense] Pulling in opposite directions.

[whispering, tense] The fabric stretches… the horses strain… [triumphant] and the pants hold.

[excited] Miners line up around the block.

[warm] He teams up with a fabric merchant named Levi Strauss.

[serious, slower] Without the rivets? [ominous] Your favorite jeans might never exist.

[softly, smiling] Look at your jeans. Those tiny metal dots? That's him.
```
