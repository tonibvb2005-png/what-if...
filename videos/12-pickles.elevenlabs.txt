[intense] A dog saved the World Cup.

[curious] No, seriously. This really happened.

March nineteen sixty-six. Four months before the World Cup in England, the trophy is on display at an exhibition in London.

[tense] Then one Sunday... it's gone. Stolen. In broad daylight.

England is in shock. The newspapers go crazy. [pause] Then the head of the FA gets a ransom note. Fifteen thousand pounds... or the trophy is gone forever.

[tense] Police catch a man at the handover. But the trophy? Still missing.

[softly] One week later, a man called David Corbett takes his dog for a walk in South London. The dog's name... is Pickles.

[building] Pickles starts sniffing at a hedge. There's a package, wrapped in newspaper.

[whispers] David opens it... [pause] [excited] and finds the World Cup!

[amazed] Four months later, England win the World Cup at Wembley.

[playful] And Pickles? He gets a medal, a movie role... and an invitation to the team's celebration party.

[softly] The greatest hero of the nineteen sixty-six World Cup... might have been a dog. [pause] Follow for more football stories.
