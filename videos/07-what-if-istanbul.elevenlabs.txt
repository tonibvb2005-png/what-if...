[curious] What if you left the craziest final in football history... at half-time?

May twenty-fifth, two thousand five. Istanbul. Liverpool against AC Milan, in the Champions League final.

[disbelief] Not even one minute in... Paolo Maldini scores. One nil, Milan.

Then Hernan Crespo scores. [pause] And scores again. [sighs] Three nil at half-time.

[deadpan] His mate has seen enough. He grabs his coat... and goes home.

[softly] But our guy stays. And thousands of Liverpool fans start singing... You'll Never Walk Alone.

[building] Fifty-four minutes. Steven Gerrard heads one in. Three one.

[excited] Two minutes later... Vladimir Shmeetser, from distance. Three two!

[tense] Then a penalty for Liverpool. Shabby Alonso... saved! [shouting] But he smashes in the rebound! Three three! In six minutes!

[whispers] Extra time. Shevchenko, from close range... [pause] and Yerzy Dudek makes a double save nobody can explain.

[tense] Penalties. Dudek dances on his line like a madman. [pause] Shevchenko steps up...

[shouting] SAVED! LIVERPOOL ARE CHAMPIONS OF EUROPE!

[playful] And his mate? [pause] He heard the whole comeback... on the radio, on the bus home. [pause] Follow for more What Ifs.
