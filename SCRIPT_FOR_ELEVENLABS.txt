If something jumps on your back at night… don't turn around. In the dark forests of Germany, there's a legend old farmers still whisper about. They call it the Aufhocker. The one who leaps onto you. It waits at lonely crossroads, near old graveyards and wayside crosses. When a traveler walks home alone after dark… it follows him. Silently. Then, without warning… it jumps onto his back. And it never lets go. With every step, it grows heavier. And heavier. Until the man collapses under the weight, somewhere on the road. His only hope is to reach the first house of the village… or hear the church bells ring. Because at the first light of morning, the Aufhocker lets go. But some travelers made it home… and never told anyone what they carried. So if you're ever walking alone at night, and your back starts to feel heavy… keep walking. Don't look back.
