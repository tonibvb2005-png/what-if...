## 2. Text zum Einfügen – Eleven v3 (mit Tags)

```
[curious] He painted his lighthouse like a candy cane, and that's exactly why this ship is still afloat.

[casual] You see, there are two lighthouses on this coast. Both snow white, both the same height. One guards the safe harbor. [ominous] The other guards a field of razor-sharp rocks.

[casual] So one morning, the new keeper hauls ten buckets of red paint up to the tower. [amused] The old keeper almost chokes on his coffee. [sarcastic, grumpy old man] "Are we running a lighthouse... or a carnival?"

[matter-of-fact] But he paints stripes anyway. [rhythmic] Red, white, red, white, all the way around the tower. [amused] The whole town laughs at him. The fishermen even throw rotten fish at his door.

[serious] Then fall comes. [quietly] The fog is so thick, a freighter can't even see its own bow. [tense] Suddenly, a white tower appears. The captain turns toward the harbor. [building tension] But then the fog tears open. [sharp] Red. White. Red. [urgent] And he yanks the wheel around, just feet from the rocks.

[curious] So how did he know? [explaining] Well, it's called a daymark. At night, every lighthouse has its own flash pattern. But in daylight, one white tower looks just like any other. That's why, to this day, every tower gets its own paint job.

[playful] And the old keeper? [deadpan] He's painting checkers.
```
