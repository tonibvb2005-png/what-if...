If you find footprints in the forest leading away from you… you're walking toward her. High in the mountains of the Dominican Republic, people warn you never to follow footprints at night. Because of her. La Ciguapa. A woman with eyes that shine in the dark… and hair so long it covers her whole body. But look at her feet. They face backwards. Every step she takes leaves a trail that points the wrong way. Hunters who tracked her walked deeper and deeper into the mountains… and never came back. Some say she only appears under the full moon. And she doesn't speak. She whimpers… like a lost puppy, so you'll come closer. So if you're ever lost in the mountains at night, and you see fresh footprints leading ahead of you… the one who made them is right behind you.
