If you see a black dog with burning eyes on a lonely road… you won't live the year. In the east of England, along the dark coast roads, people have feared this dog for centuries. They call him Black Shuck. His name comes from an old word for demon. He's as big as a horse. His fur is black as night… and his eyes burn like fire. He walks beside lonely travelers, without a single sound. No footsteps. No breath. But on the fourth of August, fifteen seventy-seven, a terrible storm hit the town of Bungay. The people ran into the church to pray. Then, the doors flew open. A black dog ran between them, through the darkness of the church. Two people kneeling in prayer died where they knelt. That same day, he appeared in another church, in Blythburgh. The steeple crashed through the roof. And as he left, he burned his claw marks into the door. Those marks are still there today. You can touch them. So if you're ever walking a lonely road at night, and you hear something padding beside you… don't turn your head. Never meet its eyes.
