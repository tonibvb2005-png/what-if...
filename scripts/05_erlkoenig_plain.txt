If you hear a voice in the forest calling your child's name… ride faster. In German folklore, a father rides through the night, his little son in his arms. Then the boy whispers. Father… don't you see him? The Elf King. A tall shape between the trees, with a crown and a long cloak. The father looks… only fog. Then the voice comes. Soft. Sweet. Come with me, child. My daughters will dance with you. The boy screams. Father, he's reaching for me! It's just the wind in the willows, the father says. But he rides faster. The voice turns cold. If you won't come willingly… I'll take you. The boy cries out one last time. Father, he's hurting me! The father reaches the farm. He looks down at the boy in his arms… the child is dead. So if your child ever says someone is calling from the trees… don't tell them it's the wind.
