If you hear chewing coming from inside a grave… the whole village is in danger. In old German villages, when sickness came, people feared something worse than the sickness. They called it the Nachzehrer. The one who doesn't rest in his grave. It began with one funeral. Then another. And another. Always the same family. At night, people heard a strange sound from the graveyard. Chewing. They believed the man in the grave was chewing on his own burial shroud… and with every bite, another relative grew weaker. So the villagers opened the grave. One of his eyes was open. To stop him, they placed a stone in his mouth… so he could never chew again. Archaeologists have found old graves like this all over Europe. With stones in their mouths. So if you ever walk past an old graveyard at night, and you hear a soft, slow chewing… don't stop to listen. He's still hungry.
