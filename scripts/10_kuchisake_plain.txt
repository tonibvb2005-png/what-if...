In nineteen seventy-nine, children in Japan were too afraid to walk home alone. Because of her. Rumors spread from town to town. Schools sent kids home in groups, and police patrolled the streets. They were looking for a woman in a long coat and a white mask. Kuchisake-onna. She waits on street corners after dark and asks one question. Am I beautiful? If you say no… she won't let you leave. If you say yes… she slowly pulls down her mask. Her smile stretches from ear to ear. Even now? she asks. Whatever you answer… she makes you look just like her. Running doesn't help. She's always waiting at the next corner. Some say the only way out is to tell her she looks average. While she's confused… you run. So if a woman in a mask ever stops you on your way home… think very carefully before you answer.
