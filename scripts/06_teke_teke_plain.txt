If you hear a sound like teke, teke, teke behind you at night… don't run. In Japan, they tell the story of a young woman who fell onto the railway tracks on a freezing winter night. The train never stopped. But she didn't die right away. In the freezing cold, she crawled… searching for what she had lost. Now her spirit haunts the stations. They call her Teke Teke. That's the sound she makes. Her elbows dragging over the ground. Teke. Teke. Teke. She moves faster than you can run. Much faster. Some say she asks you a question. Where are my legs? If you can't answer… she makes you just like her. So if you're waiting for the last train, and you hear something dragging along the platform… don't look down.
