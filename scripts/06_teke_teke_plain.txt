If you hear a sound like teke, teke, teke behind you at night… don't run. In Japan, they tell the story of a girl who fell onto the railway tracks on a freezing winter night. The train cut her in half. But the cold froze her wounds. She didn't die right away. She crawled, screaming, looking for her legs. Now her spirit haunts the stations. They call her Teke Teke. That's the sound she makes. Her elbows dragging over the ground. Teke. Teke. Teke. She moves faster than you can run. Much faster. Some say she asks you a question. Where are my legs? If you can't answer… she cuts you in half too. Just like her. So if you're waiting for the last train, and you hear something dragging along the platform… don't look down.
