If a dog with burning red eyes starts following you home at night… pray it's white. In Central America, they say two spirits walk the dark roads after midnight. Both look like huge dogs. They're called El Cadejo. The white one protects lonely travelers. It walks beside you… and keeps the darkness away. But the black one… hunts. Its eyes glow like embers. Instead of paws, it has the hooves of a goat. And when it walks, you hear chains… dragging over the stones. It follows people who are out too late. It never makes a sound… until it's right behind you. Some say if it touches you, you lose your mind. Others say you simply disappear. So if you're walking home at night, and you hear chains behind you… don't run. It only chases what runs.
