If you hear a woman crying outside your window at night… don't open it. In Ireland, for hundreds of years, families have feared one sound more than any other. The scream of the Banshee. She appears as a pale woman with long silver hair… and eyes red from crying. She doesn't kill. She warns. When she screams outside a house, someone inside won't live to see the morning. People say she combs her long hair with a silver comb. And if you ever find that comb lying on the road… never pick it up. Because she always comes back for it. So tonight, if you hear soft crying outside, and you notice an old silver comb on your windowsill… she's already here.
