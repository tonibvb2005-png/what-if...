In twelve eighty-four, a hundred and thirty children followed a song… and never came back. In the German town of Hamelin, this isn't just a fairy tale. It's written in the town's own records. That summer, the town was overrun by rats. They were everywhere. In the grain, in the houses… in the cradles. Then a stranger arrived, dressed in many colors. The Pied Piper. He promised to rid the town of every rat… for a price. He played his pipe, and the rats followed him into the river. Every single one drowned. But the townspeople refused to pay. On the twenty-sixth of June, he returned. Dressed as a hunter. And he played a different song. This time, it wasn't the rats who followed. It was the children. A hundred and thirty of them danced through the streets, out of the town gate… and into a mountain. Only two came back. One was blind. The other couldn't speak. To this day, there's a street in Hamelin where no music may be played. So if you ever hear a pipe playing at night, and your feet start to move on their own… stop. Don't follow the music.
